The interesting problems aren't the prompts; they're getting retrieval to surface the right thing, keeping answers grounded so the model doesn't make stuff up, writing evals so I know when something breaks, and keeping cost and latency sane under load.

Right now I'm an SMTS at Salesforce, building LLM-backed services and AI-assisted operational workflows. The work combines approved observability, source, and knowledge systems, then turns that context into concise, reviewable answers for engineers.

All of that sits on 6+ years of backend work where things fall over if you get them wrong. At Games24x7 I built high-traffic event-driven systems for trust, risk, onboarding, and autoscaling. The common thread is reliability: clear failure modes, measurable behavior, and systems that remain understandable under pressure.
