📋 BACKSTORY

AI work, grounded in real systems.

Lately I spend most of my time on AI agents and LLM systems — agents that call tools, RAG pipelines, and Slack bots that do actual work. The interesting problems aren't the prompts; they're getting retrieval to surface the right thing, keeping answers grounded so the model doesn't make stuff up, writing evals so I know when something breaks, and keeping cost and latency sane under load.

Right now I'm an SMTS at Salesforce, building LLM-backed services and an on-call AI bot that handles the first pass of root-cause analysis. When something breaks, it digs through logs in Splunk, traces the code in GitHub, and pulls in context from Google Drive, Confluence, and Slack — then hands the on-call engineer a single summary of what likely went wrong. It holds up at ~1.5K RPS with sub-second p95 on retrieval.

All of that sits on 6+ years of backend work where things fall over if you get them wrong. At Games24x7 I built systems that took 2.7K RPS during the IPL, ran 200K+ KYC checks a day, and held up for 10M concurrent users — event-driven pipelines, fraud detection, KYC automation, and autoscaling, all built to stay up.
